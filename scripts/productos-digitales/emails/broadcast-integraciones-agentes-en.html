Until today, everything you created in AI Chef Pro ended the same way: copy, paste, and open another tab to send it, save it or post it. Not anymore. Your specialized agents now work inside your own tools: Gmail, Google Sheets, Instagram, Notion, Canva and more — 16 in total.

It works in three steps, and the third one isn’t yours:

  1. Just ask, right in the chat: “send this recipe via Gmail,” “put this table into Google Sheets,” “post this photo to Instagram.” No commands to learn, no settings to configure first.

  3. The agent does it: the email goes out, the spreadsheet gets created, the photo gets posted. And the authorization stays saved until it expires.
