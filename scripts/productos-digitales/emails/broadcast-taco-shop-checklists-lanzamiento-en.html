A new kit just landed in our English store: Taco Shop Checklists Kit Pro. The tasks are already written: you adjust them to your shop, print, delegate and file.

It’s 11 Excel checklists with 298 taco shop tasks, sorted by shift, area and role. Every task has its area and who does it, and the counter on each list skips the tasks you mark N/A. Temperatures are in °F under the FDA Food Code (41 °F cold, 135 °F hot), and every sheet prints on US Letter.

Inside the kit:

  • Opening and closing: walk-in and freezer temperatures, the trompo lit, the griddle and comal calibrated, the cold salsa bar, guisados in the steam table, the counter and the delivery apps; at close, where the leftover trompo meat goes, the cash count and a written tip pool.
  • The trompo for al pastor: marinade, the cone checked as received, stacking, the outer layer probed at 145 °F (or your county’s figure if it’s stricter) at the start and middle of service, carving only the cooked layer and the yield in tacos per pound.
  • Salsa bar and allergens: each salsa timed on and off the bar at 41 °F or below, restocked in small batches and never topped off, the salsa macha (peanuts and/or sesame) kept apart with its own spoon, flour tortillas apart from corn and the 9 major allergens.
  • Nixtamal, tortillas and guisados: the corn mill cleaned and sanitized before and after grinding, masa held as a TCS food, the tortilla machine calibrated, and guisados held at 135 °F and reheated to 165 °F within 2 hours.
  • Manager and team: daily and weekly manager lists (orders to your Mexican food distributor, taco costing, time-clock records), duties for 6 roles from the taquero to the delivery driver, and weekly and monthly cleaning with gas checks and hood filters.
  • Bonuses: a pre-shift meeting template, an annual calendar with 27 dates and yearly tasks, from Three Kings Day and Super Bowl Sunday to Cinco de Mayo, Day of the Dead and the posadas, and the US · UK · Canada · Australia Hospitality Equivalents Guide, if you’re reading from outside the States.
