A new kit just landed in our English store: Burger Joint Checklists Kit Pro. It’s for burger joint owners, shift managers and grill cooks tired of repeating the same instructions every shift: the griddle heated up late, the fryer oil nobody filtered, the make line that ran out mid-rush, the delivery app left on after close and the cash drawer that didn’t match. It’s 11 Excel checklists with 346 burger joint tasks, sorted by shift, area and role. Every task has who does it and when, and the counter on each list skips the tasks you mark N/A. Temperatures are in °F under the FDA Food Code (the walk-in and the make line at 41 °F or below, ground beef cooked to 155 °F and checked with a probe), and every sheet prints on US Letter.

Inside the kit:

  • Opening and closing: kitchen and front of house, with delivery and takeout, plus a whole-restaurant checklist (alarm, lights, POS, the griddles and fryers, with the alarm and lock-up last) and a cash drawer count with the over/short against the POS Z report.
  • The grill, the make line and the fry station: the griddle at 400–450 °F, smash burgers, cheese melted and buns toasted, the make line set up in order, refilled and broken down, and the fryer oil at 350–355 °F, tested with a TPM tester and filtered the way your fryer maker says.
  • Manager and roles: daily, weekly and monthly checklists with a shift handover, and a checklist each for the grill cook, line cook, fry cook, shift manager and server.
  • Cleaning, inventory and maintenance: a daily inventory with reorder flags, a weekly deep clean, a FIFO check with a freezer shelf-life table, monthly griddle and fryer maintenance and a quarterly and annual sheet for contracted services, from hood and duct cleaning (NFPA 96) to fire extinguishers and the used fryer oil pickup.
  • Event checklists (game night, Burger Week, the holiday season, patio season), 3 blank master templates, a pre-shift meeting template and a calendar with 22 key US dates for burger joints, from Super Bowl Sunday and National Burger Month to New Year’s Eve.
