Inside the kit:

  • Setup and teardown: parking only in a legal vending spot, the generator outside and away from the window, propane pressure, cold equipment at 41 °F or below, the POS and starting bank; at teardown, leftovers cooled and date-marked, wastewater dumped only at the commissary, the cash close-out and the truck secured for the road.
