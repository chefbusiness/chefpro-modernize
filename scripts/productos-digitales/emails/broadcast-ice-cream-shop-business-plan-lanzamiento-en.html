A new product just landed in our English store: Ice Cream Shop Business Plan Kit. It’s for anyone who wants to open a neighborhood ice cream shop that makes its own ice cream, with scoops, pints and ice cream cakes, and needs to know whether it works on paper before signing a lease or ordering a batch freezer.

It’s 3 files: a 10-section business plan in Word, an Excel financial model with 9 sheets and an 80-task startup checklist, set up for the US with notes for the UK.

Three things you’ll use from day one:

  • A plan that’s already written. Executive summary, concept, market and competition, marketing, operations, staffing, financials, permits and licenses, and an action plan, written for a neighborhood ice cream shop in the format lenders expect. Its figures are the ones in the Excel model, and it’s honest about them: a net margin of about 6% in year 1, not the 10-25% many articles quote.
  • Your numbers, with the winter in them. Startup costs line by line, from the batch freezer, the hardening cabinet and the dipping cabinets to the build-out, a 3-year P&L driven by customers per day and average check, break-even in customers per day, three scenarios, staffing with seasonal scoopers and employer payroll taxes, and a 12-month cash flow that starts in March, so you see the July peak and the January dip before you sign the lease.
  • Buy a base or make your own mix? The example buys a pasteurized base from a licensed dairy; making your own mix is a variant. States handle it differently, so the plan never says “no license needed”: it tells you to ask your state department of agriculture and your health department, and the checklist turns the decision into a task.
  • An 80-task startup checklist. Six phases: LLC and EIN, business license and sales tax permit, plan review, building permit, the base supplier agreement, running water for the dipper wells, the 9 allergens including sesame, teen scooper hours, Opening Day, your first 90 days and the winter plan.

Plus three bonuses: a Dairy License, Permits & Food Safety Guide (US + UK notes), US ice cream shop benchmarks, each with its source or marked as a kit estimate, and the US · UK · Canada · Australia Equivalents Guide.

The files are Microsoft Excel and Word, set up to print on US Letter. Health, dairy and sales tax rules vary by state and county, so the checklist starts by asking yours, and the kit is a planning tool, not financial, tax or legal advice: review the final numbers with your accountant before you apply for a loan.
