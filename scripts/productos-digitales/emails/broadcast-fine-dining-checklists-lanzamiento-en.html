Every checklist has a counter that skips the tasks you mark N/A and a sign-off line, and the cash drawer file counts by US bill and coin. Temperatures are in °F under the FDA Food Code, and every sheet prints on US Letter.

Inside the kit:

  • Safety and the inspector: parasite destruction and the consumer advisory for raw fish, sous vide stored in the bag only with a HACCP plan or variance, the 9 major allergens, and a Quarterly & Annual sheet of contracted services.
  • Wine, events and the house: pairing course by course with a non-alcoholic option, the wine cellar, the chef’s table, pop-ups and wine dinners, the whole restaurant’s opening and closing, and a cash drawer count with automatic over/short.
