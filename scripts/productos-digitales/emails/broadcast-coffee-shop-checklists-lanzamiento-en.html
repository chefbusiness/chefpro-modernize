A new kit just landed in our English store: Coffee Shop Checklists Kit Pro. The tasks are already written: you adjust them to your shop, print, delegate and file.

Every task has who does it, when and initials, and the counter on each list skips the tasks you mark N/A. Temperatures are in °F under the FDA Food Code (open milk and the pastry case at 41 °F or below), and every sheet prints on US Letter.

Inside the kit:

  • Cash drawer opening and closing: a count by US bill and coin, starting bank and the over/short against the POS Z report, worked out for you.
  • Event checklists (Sunday brunch, Valentine’s Day, the holiday season, patio season), 3 blank master templates, a pre-shift meeting template and a calendar with 22 key US dates for coffee shops, National Coffee Day included.
