A new kit just landed in our English store: Chocolate Shop Checklists Kit Pro. It’s for owners, chocolatiers and shift leads of shops that make their own bonbons and bars and are tired of repeating the same instructions every shift: a batch tempered in a kitchen that was too warm, bonbons that bloomed in the display case, a fresh-cream ganache left out with the rest, the same tongs going from the hazelnut tray to the plain one, and a holiday rush nobody planned for. The tasks are already written: you adjust them to your shop, print, delegate and file.

It’s 11 Excel checklists with 338 chocolate shop tasks, sorted by shift, area and role. Every task has its area, who does it and the time, and the counter on each list skips the tasks you mark N/A. Temperatures are in °F (the kitchen at 64–68 °F, the cool room at 59–64 °F, the bonbon display case at 60–64 °F, and fresh-cream ganache in the fridge at 41 °F or below unless a lab test says otherwise), and every sheet prints on US Letter.

Inside the kit:

  • Opening and closing: the kitchen, the display case and the counter, with each temperature logged and an allergen notice on every tray, plus a whole-shop checklist (alarm, lights, HVAC, the display cases switched on first thing and off only once the product is stored) and a cash drawer count with sales by the pound and the over/short against the POS Z report.
  • Tempering and molding: typical curves for dark, milk and white chocolate (your couverture maker’s chart always rules), a temper test and batch log, bonbon shells, bars with nut and peanut runs last, labels and a shelf-life table by product type.
  • Manager and roles: daily, weekly and monthly checklists (pre-orders, temperatures, waste, food cost, time records) and a checklist each for the chocolatier, the sales associate and the shift lead.
  • Cleaning and maintenance: a weekly deep clean and stock count, monthly tasks and a quarterly and annual sheet for contracted services and paperwork, from lab tests for new ganaches and refrigeration by an EPA 608 technician to your health permit and any state candy license.
  • Seasons: the holiday season (with Hanukkah gelt and your shipping cutoff), Valentine’s Day and Easter, 3 blank master templates, a pre-shift huddle template and a 12-month US calendar, from Valentine’s Day and Mother’s Day to Sweetest Day and Thanksgiving.
  • The US · UK · Canada · Australia Hospitality Equivalents Guide, if you’re reading from outside the States.
