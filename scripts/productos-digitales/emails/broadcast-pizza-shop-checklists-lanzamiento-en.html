It’s for pizza shop owners, shift managers and pizza makers tired of repeating the same instructions every shift: the oven fired up late, the dough balls nobody took out of the walk-in, the make line that ran out mid-rush, the delivery app left on after close and the cash drawer that didn’t match. It’s 11 Excel checklists with 373 pizza shop tasks, sorted by shift, area and role. Every task has who does it and when, and the counter on each list skips the tasks you mark N/A. Temperatures are in °F under the FDA Food Code (the walk-in and the make line at 41 °F or below, hot food in the delivery bag at 135 °F or above), and every sheet prints on US Letter.

Inside the kit:

  • Opening and closing: kitchen and front of house, with the patio, delivery and takeout, plus a whole-shop checklist (alarm, lights, POS, the oven preheat and cool-down) and a cash drawer count with the over/short against the POS Z report.
  • The pizza oven station and the make line: wood-fired or deck oven in °F (750–840 °F for Neapolitan), bake times, dough tempered before service and made for tomorrow at close, the make line set up in order, refilled and broken down, and apps and pasta.
  • Manager and roles: daily, weekly and monthly checklists with a shift handover, and a checklist each for the pizza maker, line cook, shift manager, server and delivery driver.
  • Cleaning, inventory and maintenance: a daily inventory with reorder flags, a weekly deep clean, a FIFO check with a freezer shelf-life table, monthly oven maintenance and a quarterly and annual sheet for contracted services, from hood and chimney cleaning (NFPA 96) to fire extinguishers.
  • Event checklists (game night and the Super Bowl delivery rush, Valentine’s Day, the holiday season, patio season), 3 blank master templates, a pre-shift meeting template and a calendar with 22 key US dates for pizza shops, from Super Bowl Sunday to Thanksgiving Eve and New Year’s Eve.
