A new kit just landed in our English store: Catering Checklists Kit Pro. It’s for caterers, event managers and catering chefs tired of improvising at every event: the BEO that changed at the last minute, the hotel pans that went out without a temperature reading, the venue that didn’t have a handwashing sink, the extra staff who don’t know what to do and the balance nobody chased. The tasks are already written: you adjust them to your operation, print, delegate and file.

It’s 11 Excel checklists with 347 catering and event tasks, sorted by event phase, area and role. Every task has who does it and when, and the counter on each list skips the tasks you mark N/A. Temperatures are in °F under the FDA Food Code (hot food at 135 °F or above and cold at 41 °F or below, from loading to the buffet), and every sheet prints on US Letter.

Inside the kit:

  • Production and transport: advance planning from the BEO, day-before prep, event-day cooking and packing in hotel pans and insulated carriers, then loading, a temperature reading every 30 minutes on the road and unloading at the venue.
  • Setup, service and breakdown: the temporary kitchen with a handwashing station and the open-flame rules for chafing fuel, room and buffet setup with allergen signs, a final check before guests arrive, and the load-out with leftovers handled by the cooling rule.
  • Event manager and roles: event week, event day and the wrap-up, plus a quarterly and annual sheet for permits, insurance with the COI and contracted services; and a checklist each for the event chef, banquet captain, server and bartender.
  • Deposits, invoicing and payments: what to close before and after each event, a settlement with sales tax at your own rate, the balance due with an OVERDUE status, and a billing log for 25 events. The mandatory service charge stays apart from the gratuity.
  • Event checklists (weddings, corporate events, cocktail receptions, outdoor events), a commissary opening and closing checklist, 3 blank master templates, a pre-event briefing template and a calendar with 22 key US dates for caterers, from wedding season to holiday parties.
  • The US · UK · Canada · Australia Hospitality Equivalents Guide, if you’re reading from outside the States.
