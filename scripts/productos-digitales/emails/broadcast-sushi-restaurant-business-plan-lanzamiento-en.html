Four things you’ll use from day one:

  • A plan that’s already written. Executive summary, concept, market and competition, marketing, operations, staffing, financials, permits and an action plan, in the format lenders expect for a neighborhood sushi restaurant. Its figures are the ones in the Excel model.

There’s also a bonus: the US · UK · Canada · Australia Equivalents Guide, so terms, units and rules translate if you work across markets.
