## BLOQUEANTE (1)

**B1. docx §9 [102]: permiso legal atribuido a Missouri que la fuente no da y que Montana contradice.** Texto: «The conditions are simple. The host buys and owns the alcohol, and leftovers go back to the host. If you buy it, you act only as their agent, authorized in writing, at cost with receipts and no markup.» MO 24-01 no habla de comprar como agente; define venta como «the exchange of money for a commodity» e incluye los recargos «for intoxicating liquor or its delivery» (cobrarle al anfitrión el alcohol que compraste es eso). MT exige que lo compre el anfitrión «separately». La cláusula viene de brewerslaw (FL, marcada «AI Generated», research §6.1). Arreglo (texto): sustituir esas dos frases por «The conditions are simple. The host buys and owns the alcohol, and leftovers go back to the host. Do not buy the alcohol for the host and get paid back: in Missouri, money for alcohol or its delivery can count as a sale, and Montana requires the host to buy it separately.» Sin cifras.
