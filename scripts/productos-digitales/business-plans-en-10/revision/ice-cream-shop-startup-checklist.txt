B10	Written food safety plan (receiving ≤ 41 °F, lot codes, date marking)
C10	Consultant / Owner
D10	Before opening
E10	Receive the base at 41 °F (5 °C) or colder with lot codes and date marking. After the dairy's pasteurization you add only flavorings and mix-ins: no raw eggs, no raw milk or cream. If you pasteurize your own mix (the variant), fats, egg yolks and emulsifiers go in BEFORE pasteurization (155 °F for 30 minutes or 175 °F for 25 seconds), never after
A11	☐
B11	Batch freezing, hardening ≤ 0 °F, dipping cabinet 6-10 °F
C11	Owner
D11	Before opening
E11	Batch-freeze (draw at 16-19 °F), harden to 0 °F (−18 °C) or colder at the core, and hold the dipping cabinet at 6-10 °F or the manufacturer's range. Store at 0 °F or colder and never refreeze melted ice cream. Your food safety plan sets the cabinet temperature
A12	☐
B12	Custard flavors from a custard base, no raw eggs
C12	Owner
D12	Before opening
E12	Custard flavors need a custard base from your dairy: never add raw eggs, raw milk or cream after the base has been pasteurized
A13	☐
B13	Lot traceability, date marking and temperature logs
C13	Owner
D13	From the first batch
E13	Lot and date of each batch, delivery temperature of the base, freezer and cabinet temperatures, and the supplier of each ingredient
A14	☐
B14	Recipe cards for each flavor with its allergens
C14	Ice cream maker
D14	Before opening
E14	Recipe, yield and allergens of every flavor: milk, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts, wheat (cookies, cones), soy and sesame
A15	☐
B15	Allergen sign and information at the counter
C15	Owner
D15	Before opening
E15	The 9 major allergens include sesame (UK: 14). Post a sign or menu board and train staff to answer. Never promise "allergen-free": scoops, tubs and dipper wells are shared
A16	☐
B16	Complete label on packaged pints
C16	Owner
D16	Before selling packaged pints
E16	Pints you leave packaged in the freezer need a label (Food Code 3-602.11): name of the food, ingredients, allergens, net quantity and your name and address
A17	☐
B17	Menu names that meet 21 CFR 135.110 ("ice cream", "frozen custard")
C17	Ice cream maker
D17	Before opening
E17	Use "ice cream" only if the recipe has at least 10% milkfat and weighs 4.5 lb per gallon or more, and "frozen custard" only with 1.4% egg yolk solids. Otherwise name it by flavor or use another description. If you call it ice cream, the federal standard applies, gelato included
