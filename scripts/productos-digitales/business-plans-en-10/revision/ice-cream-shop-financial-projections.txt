A56	DSCR is calculated BEFORE debt and AFTER tax: income tax is subtracted from pre-tax income, depreciation and interest are added back, and the result is divided by what has to be paid that year. Below the minimum in the "0. Assumptions" tab, the business does not generate enough to repay the loan. In the schedule, the available cash flow from year 4 on is held at year 3's: the P&L projects only three years, and no growth is invented here that has not been calculated.
