B2     : 02 · Tasting Menu Mise en Place Checklist: Long, Mid & Same-Day Prep + Plating Bible
B4     : How to use this template:
B5     : ▸ Organize the mise en place by course of the tasting menu.
B6     : ▸ Separate prep by lead time.
B7     : ▸ Control every plating component with the plating bible.
B8     : ▸ Adapt the sheet to the number of courses on your menu (7–15).
B10    : Prep lead times:
B11    : ▸ Long-lead (24–72 hr): stocks, ferments, marinades
B12    : ▸ Mid-lead (6–12 hr): sous vide, infusions, gels
B13    : ▸ Same-day (2–4 hr): foams, hot sauces, fresh cuts
B14    : ▸ Minute zero: microgreens, flowers, snows, crisps
B16    : Raw fish and other products that go to the plate raw:
B17    : ▸ Any fish served raw or undercooked (tartare, ceviche, crudo, marinated, cured or cold-smoked, that is, not reaching 145 °F) needs parasite destruction by freezing (FDA Food Code 3-402.11): at −4 °F or below for 7 days, or at −31 °F until solid and held 15 hr, or at −31 °F until solid and then 24 hr at −4 °F. Keep the supplier's letter or record for 90 days (3-402.12) and print the consumer advisory on the menu (3-603.11). The citrus acid in ceviche does NOT replace freezing.
B18    : ▸ Microgreens, edible flowers and herbs go raw to the pass with no heat step afterward: wash them under running potable water (if you use a produce wash or chlorine, only one approved for fruits and vegetables, at the label dose) and RINSE them before they enter the plating station (FDA Food Code 3-302.15).
