B14    : ▸ In "Cash Count by Denomination" fill in ONLY the "Quantity" column (number of bills or coins, whole number): the subtotal and TOTAL CASH calculate themselves. In the UK, Canada or Australia, unprotect the sheet (no password) and change the label and value of each row.

B21    : ▸ One row per day: enter starting bank, sales by payment type, POS Z report and deposit; Total Collected and Over/Short calculate themselves and the over/short turns amber.
