B2     : 05 · Chocolate Shop Cleaning Checklist, Inventory & Maintenance Schedule
B4     : How to use this template:
B5     : ▸ Deep clean, equipment maintenance, periodic inventories.
B6     : ▸ Schedule these tasks on the weekly/monthly calendar.
B7     : ▸ Mark ✓ when each task is done.
B9     : Customization:
B10    : ▸ Adjust frequencies to your production volume.
B11    : ▸ Add tasks specific to your kitchen.
B13    : Beyond the month:
