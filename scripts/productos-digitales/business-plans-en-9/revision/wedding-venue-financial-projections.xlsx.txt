## Instructions
A1: HOW TO USE THIS WORKBOOK — Wedding Venue Financial Projections
A3: HOW TO USE THIS WORKBOOK
A4: This financial plan is a MODEL: you type only in the GREEN cells and everything else recalculates on its own.
A5: 1. "0. Assumptions" is the control sheet: guests per event, revenue per guest excl. sales tax, events per year, the mix of venue rental & services and caterer commission, direct event cost, rent, financing and taxes.
A6: 2. Startup cost items, fixed costs and staffing are typed in their own tab, also in green. No number is typed twice.
A7: 3. Only "Break-Even" is derived entirely from the rest. The other three tabs also have green cells you can edit: the pessimistic and optimistic scenarios in "Scenarios", seasonality and the launch ramp-up in "12-Month Cash Flow", and the other funding sources in "Financing" (rows 8-11 count as sources but are not amortized: Your bank or SBA 7(a) loan goes in "Loan requested" on "0. Assumptions"). Everything else is calculated.
A8: 4. All figures are EXCL. SALES TAX. To turn a price that includes sales tax into the pre-tax price, divide by 1 + your sales tax rate. The venue bills venue rental & services (8% example rate) and the caterer commission (0%); the caterer bills the menu and the bar to the couple, and they do not enter this workbook. The rate of each sales line is in the sales tax column of the P&L, and from it come the collections and the sales tax collected. The workbook assumes you file and remit quarterly; if your state asks for monthly returns, adjust the timing.
