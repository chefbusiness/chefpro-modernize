## How to use AI as a chef

Use it on one dish the brigade already sends. Not on a concept menu. Not on a week of specials. One card, finished, that the next shift can cook without asking you.

Give the agent what the invoice says, not what you hope the dish costs. Gross weight, the yield after trim, the pack price. Ask for the costed card. Then put the supplier label next to the allergen line and correct it. Then ask for the production list for the covers you actually expect. Then the purchase order, in the pack the supplier sells.

Taste it. Sign the card. If the agent wrote a time you have never used, the time on the card is yours, not the model’s.

That is how to use AI as a chef: costing, the menu, allergens, production, purchasing. Five jobs, one dish, then the next dish.

| Kitchen job | What you give the agent | What you check before service |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Recipe costing | Invoice price, gross weight, yield after trim | The cost against this week’s invoice |
| Menu | The costed cards, and which dishes you already sell | That the dish still belongs on the menu you print |
| Allergens | The recipe, and the supplier label | The label itself, not the agent’s memory of a brand |
| Production | Covers, and the portion written on the card | The prep list against what is actually in the fridge |
| Purchasing | Gross weight times those covers | The order against the supplier’s pack size |

## AI tips for chefs

These are the tips that survive a Saturday night.

1. One dish. The menu is a pile of cards, not one prompt.
2. Prices and yields go in. “Make it cheaper” is not an instruction the pass can cook.
3. The supplier label decides the allergen line. A branded sauce, a stock cube and a breadcrumb mix change the card when the brand changes.
4. The card is weights, cost, allergens and method. A story about the dish belongs on the menu, if it belongs anywhere.
5. Update the card when the invoice changes. A card from last season is a souvenir.
6. Use a chatbot to unblock an idea. Use an agent when tomorrow’s shift has to plate the same dish.
7. Taste, then sign. A generated method is a draft, including the time and the temperature.
8. Count your savings on your own invoices. A figure from someone else’s kitchen says nothing about yours.

## Where a chatbot stops

A chatbot is useful. It drafts a special, a staff-meal idea, a first pass at wording (here is how to brief an [AI recipe generator from ingredients](https://aichef.pro/en/blog/ai-recipe-generator-from-ingredients) so the draft fits your line). It does not keep your yields, and it does not know this week’s invoice unless you paste it. An agent is the place those jobs live so the brigade is not starting from a blank box every service.

Keep both, and don’t ask the chatbot to do the agent’s job.
