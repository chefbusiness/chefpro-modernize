## What's Behind One Million Queries

Every time a user asks an agent something and gets a response, that counts as one query. Simple enough — but behind that single interaction there are 80 specialized agents in English, available in seven languages — Spanish, English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch — and a community that spans restaurant chefs, hospitality students, pastry chefs, bakers, ice cream makers, chocolatiers, hotel F&B directors, culinary consultants, and restaurant managers, all over the world.

The number we care about isn't the million itself. It's what it represents: hours of work given back to professionals who need them to cook, to create, to run their operations better. Every query is time that wasn't spent staring at a blank page. And that, multiplied by one million, starts to mean something real.

What we've also learned is which agents the community reaches for most, which ones raise the most questions, and where we still need to sharpen things up. That's the information driving every update we ship. ### How to Get the Most Out of the Comparison

These seven are general-purpose models: their value lies in comparing styles, contrasting approaches, and seeing how each one tackles the same problem. They are not the specialized AI Chef Pro agents, which already come with professional context built in. Here is how to use them effectively:

1. **Ask a specific, professional question.** For example: rebalancing an autumn menu with a target food cost of 30% (just an example). The more specific the question, the more useful the comparison.
2. **Run it through three different models and compare reasoning, depth, and gaps.** Not all of them think the same way or prioritize the same things. You will see real differences in how they structure their answers and what they leave out.
3. **Use Sonar Deep Research when you need sourced research.** It is the right model for analyzing market trends, studying competitors, or finding business opportunities backed by verifiable data.
4. **Use Grok when what matters is what is happening right now.** Its real-time information access makes it especially useful for current context: commodity prices, emerging trends, industry news.

The key distinction is this: general models are for exploring and comparing. Working with your kitchen's actual data — recipe costing, spec sheets, menus, business plans — is what the specialized AI Chef Pro agents are built for, with professional context loaded from the very first message. If you are unsure when it makes sense to use a general model versus a specialized agent, [this comparison between ChatGPT and AI Chef Pro](https://aichef.pro/en/chatgpt-for-restaurants) walks you through it with real-world examples.
