<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Tips for getting more out of Restaurant Manager Pro</h2>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Clear Objectives</h3>
<p>Start every prompt with a sharp ask. Instead of “help with food cost,” specify “I need a food cost breakdown by menu category so I can cut low-margin dishes and re-engineer my dinner menu for a 30% target.” That precision gets you actionable data, not generic advice.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Real Data</h3>
<p>Feed the tool actual numbers. Don’t say “we’re busy”—say “85 covers a day, $32 average check, labor at 27%.” The more operational truth you drop in, the tighter the suggestions on scheduling, menu pricing, and waste reduction will be.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Operational Context</h3>
<p>Give the AI your floor plan in words: “60-seat taqueria in Austin, continuous service 11am–11pm, 8-person BOH, 4 FOH.” That level of detail means the advice on station layout, prep lists, and shift timing fits your real world, not a textbook.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Demand Actionability</h3>
<p>Always end with “give me a step-by-step plan.” Skip the theory. Ask “list five concrete steps to cut protein waste by 15% in two weeks.” AI Chef Pro will return a numbered checklist you can tape to the walk-in, not a white paper.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Review Outputs</h3>
<p>Don’t take the first answer as gospel. Cross-check it against your line’s reality. Prompt: “Which of these waste-reduction steps work for a kitchen that does 120 covers with only two combi ovens?” The tool will tailor, but your sense-check is the final pass.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Iterate</h3>
<p>If the output is close but not perfect, don’t start over—refine. Say “Take that food-cost analysis and layer on a 5% price hike. Show me the new margins and the break-even point.” Each follow-up builds precision, like folding a sharp knife.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Compare Scenarios</h3>
<p>Always ask for side-by-side options. “Compare three pricing scenarios for my dinner menu: hold prices, raise 5%, or raise 8%. Include projected covers, revenue, and margin impact.” That gives you a financial map for your next manager meeting.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Prioritize</h3>
<p>If you’re torn between margin and quality, state it: “In the seafood section, prioritize product quality over margin.” The AI will then suggest menu adjustments that protect your reputation while keeping cost in check, not just slash.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Deadlines</h3>
<p>Tie every plan to a calendar. “I need an inventory reduction plan I can execute in four weeks, starting Monday.” That forces the AI to chunk actions into weekly sprints, turning a vague goal into a doable schedule that your KM can track.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Document</h3>
<p>Build a playbook of winning prompts.
