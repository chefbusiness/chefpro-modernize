<p>Chef Consultant Pro is AI Chef Pro’s kitchen diagnostics agent, built to mirror a veteran operations auditor with 15+ years on the line. It tackles the brutal math that keeps owners up at night: actual vs. theoretical food cost, waste by category, recipe card accuracy, and margin per plate. Whether you’re a chef-owner running a single-unit gastropub in Portland or a corporate kitchen manager overseeing five high-volume concepts in Dallas, this agent digs into your numbers, spots the leaks, and proposes operational fixes—no sugarcoating, just a straight-up, data-driven outside perspective.</p>
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why prompts matter with Chef Consultant Pro</h2>
<p>A vague prompt like 'Audit my food cost' might generate a decent checklist, but specifics—segment, volume, average check, actual vs. theoretical window, waste streams to slice by protein, dairy, produce—yield a forensic report that mirrors a third-party audit costing thousands. Ask the agent to 'Conduct a 30-day food cost deep-dive for a 90-seat Italian-American joint in Chicago, $28 avg check, break down waste by meat, cheese, dry goods, and produce; cross-check 15 recipe cards for yield errors and flag any dishes where margin falls below 18%' and you get a margin-ranking, costed-out list with re-spec fixes.</p>
<p>In a regulated environment, generic prompts miss the compliance layer. American kitchens must operate under the FDA Food Code, HACCP plans, and FALCPA allergen labeling for the 9 major allergens. Without specifying that you need a HACCP flow review, the agent might not flag cross-contact risks in prep chains or temperature log gaps. Domain-specific prompts—like 'Review my hot-holding and cooling procedures against FDA Food Code 3-501.16 and suggest corrective actions for a high-volume NYC deli'—yield concrete, inspection-ready guidance, not theoretical blather. The same applies to UK (FSA, Natasha’s Law), Australia/New Zealand (FSANZ), and India (FSSAI) contexts, where a prompt that names the regulator gets you tailored compliance advice.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Recipe Costing</h3>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Output Format</h3>
<p>Specify the output you need: a costing spreadsheet, a PDF spec sheet, a list of recommended suppliers, etc. Example: 'Output the recipe cost in a table with columns for ingredient, quantity, unit, unit cost, extended cost, and percent of total.'</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Benchmarking</h3>
<p>Request market comparisons to validate your menu pricing so you're neither too cheap nor too pricey. Example: 'Compare the proposed price for lobster risotto against the benchmark for upscale Italian restaurants in New York and San Francisco to ensure it’s competitive.'</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Waste Factors</h3>
<p>Break down waste by type so your costing reflects reality. Example: 'For a whole beef tenderloin, budget 20% trim loss for cleaning and another 12% cooking shrinkage so you’re not surprised when the yield hits the plate.'</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Supplier Sourcing</h3>
<p>Ask for region-specific supplier recommendations to keep your costs grounded. Example: 'Name three organic suppliers in the Phoenix metro area for prime beef, seasonal veg, and local EV olive oil, including typical case sizes and lead times.'</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">SOPs</h3>
<p>Build SOPs to lock in consistency shift after shift. Example: 'Create an SOP for the cold station mise en place: break down each task, prep times, holding temps (keep under 41°F per FDA Food Code), and visual quality checks before service.'</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Team Training</h3>
<p>Design training plans tailored to your team’s experience and menu demands. Example: 'Build a 4-week immersion program for a line cook team new to modern Japanese cuisine, covering knife skills, dashi fundamentals, plating, and a final tasting panel.'</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Turnaround Plan</h3>
<p>For struggling restaurants, request a full diagnostic with a prioritized action plan by impact and cost. Example: 'Assess a fusion spot with slow table turns, a 45% food cost, and quality issues; deliver a 90-day turnaround roadmap with quick wins and capital-light fixes.'</p>
