<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Tips for getting more out of Burgers Pro AI+</h2>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">BURGER TYPE</h3>
<p>Always name the style—gourmet, smash, classic diner, plant-based, or regional—so Burgers Pro AI+ zeroes in on the right technique. For example, “Best grind ratio for a Texas-style smoked brisket burger?” gets you pitmaster detail instead of generic grill advice.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">INGREDIENT DETAIL</h3>
<p>List the hero components and any high-impact accents like aged cheddar, bacon jam, or pickled jalapeños. A prompt such as “How do I stop a goat-cheese-and-fig-jam burger from reading too sweet?” forces a balance-focused answer on fat, acid, and umami.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">SERVICE STYLE</h3>
<p>State whether it’s fast-casual, fine-dining, ghost-kitchen delivery, or a high-volume food truck. “Best bun that holds for 25-minute delivery without steaming” triggers packaging and moisture-barrier solutions a dine-in-only prompt would never surface.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">COOKING METHOD</h3>
<p>Name the equipment and target doneness. “Time and surface temp for a 6 oz (170 g) smash burger on a carbon-steel flat-top vs. a cast-iron plancha” yields side-by-side Maillard-reaction data, not vague “cook until done” advice.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">TREND INTELLIGENCE</h3>
<p>Ask what’s moving in a specific segment. “Current plant-based patty trends in the fast-casual space—mushroom blends, precision-fermented heme, or whole-vegetable forward?” gives you R&D direction backed by what’s actually selling, not Pinterest guesses.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">EFFICIENCY METRICS</h3>
<p>Flag your throughput goal. “Streamline a build line for 120 covers an hour with only two cooks—what’s the tightest station layout?” gets you lean-flow diagrams, not a generic brigade list.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">HEAD-TO-HEAD COMPARISONS</h3>
<p>Pit technique, ingredient, or gear against each other. “Pro and con: forming 7 oz (198 g) patties with a ring mold versus a patty press for a 60-seat gastropub—yield, texture, speed.” The answer weighs labor cost against mouthfeel, just like your P&L does.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">GUEST PROFILE</h3>
<p>Define the demographic. “Burger builds that hit for health-tracker Gen Z—macro-friendly, bold spice, Instagrammable color” steers you away from butter-basted behemoths and toward grain bowls in bun form.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">PAIRING STRATEGY</h3>
<p>Ask for beverage or side recommendations that complement the protein and dominant condiment. “Cocktail or beer pairing for a lamb-and-harissa burger with preserved-lemon yogurt?” matches intensity and fat, not just “red wine with meat.”</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">PLATING & VISUAL</h3>
<p>Request presentation techniques matched to your concept. “High-contrast plating for a black-sesame-bun burger under expo lights—what garnishes pop on camera?” speaks to the social-media check before the first bite.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">EQUIPMENT GUIDANCE</h3>
<p>Specify volume and product. “Best charbroiler for 250 covers a day—radiant, lava-rock, or infrared—considering recovery time and gas cost” returns a capex-vs-performance analysis, not a catalog page.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">REGULATORY CONTEXT</h3>
<p>Name the jurisdiction and format. “HACCP hot-hold and cooling log for smash burgers on a food truck in Austin, TX—how to document without slowing the window” respects the FDA Food Code while keeping your ticket times real.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">CUSTOMIZATION SYSTEMS</h3>
<p>Ask how to build a “build-your-own” workflow that doesn’t kill speed. “Efficient modifier setup for a POS touchscreen with 30 toppings—how to group for least cook confusion” protects your pick-up window.</p>
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">MARKETING ANGLES</h3>
<p>Request channel-specific tactics. “Reel and story ideas for a new smash-burger ghost kitchen—hooks that show the crust, not just the melt” turns Burgers Pro AI+ into your social-media sous chef.</p>
